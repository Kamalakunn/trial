📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal: Master English vocabulary through 5 fun game modes!

🎮 Game Modes:

  • 🃏 Memory Cards: Flip cards to find matching pairs (English ↔ Indonesian).
  • 🔗 Word Matching: Connect English words with their Indonesian meanings.
  • 🎯 Guess The Word: See the Indonesian meaning, pick the correct English word.
  • ✍️ Missing Letters: Fill in the missing letters of English words.
  • ⚡ Speed Challenge: Answer as many as you can in 60 seconds!

⭐ Scoring:

  • ✅ Correct answer: +10 points
  • 🎁 Completion bonus: +20 points
  • ⚡ Speed bonus: +10 points (finish quickly)

🏆 Levels: Complete a level to unlock the next one!

💡 Tips: Use hints wisely (3 per game). Practice makes perfect!
