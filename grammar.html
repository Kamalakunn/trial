📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal: Master English grammar through 10 exciting levels!

🎮 Game Modes:

  • 📝 Grammar Quiz: Multiple choice A, B, C, D.
  • ✍️ Fill in the Blanks: Type the missing word.
  • 🔀 Sentence Scramble: Arrange words into a correct sentence.
  • 🔍 Find the Mistake: Spot the grammar error.
  • ⚔️ Grammar Battle: Fast-paced timed challenge!

⭐ Scoring:

  • ✅ Correct: +10 points
  • 🎁 Level completion: +20 bonus
  • 💯 No mistakes: +30 perfect bonus

💡 Tips: Use hints wisely (3 per level). Read the material first!
